# Design Specificity Verdict

**Category-interchangeable, with an authored skin.** The chrome is specific to this product; the commerce core is not.

Authored and genuinely good: the deep-teal satin photography, the hairline-rule editorial grid, light Heebo 200 at display sizes, and the 4C education strip where a gold Hebrew micro-label sits over a tracked Latin term over quiet body copy.

Generic, and it is the load-bearing part:

- `DiamondSearchForm.tsx` renders the same `DiamondIcon` for all six shapes.
- `ProductCard.tsx` carries no diamond information — name, subtitle, "from ₪6,500". The v4 comp card carries `GIA · 1.02ct · F · VS1` + price. Strip the Hebrew and the shipped card is a generic store tile.
- `grep -rn "GIA\|IGI" src/` returns exactly one hit (`catalog.ts:60`), inside a collapsed `details`.
- `/diamond-search` is a 4-field form that opens WhatsApp, where the handoff specifies a live stone inventory with facets, result count, per-stone specs and prices.
- `.difference-grid` runs the identical gold diamond icon three times in a column.
- 364 real product images with metal variants sit unused in `public/images/products/catalog/`; the site ships 7 invented products.

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs` returned **zero findings** on both `src/` and the current handoff files (exit 0). The detector was validated against a synthetic bad file (2 findings) and a nested directory (4 findings), confirming the zero is real and not a silent no-op. One finding on the superseded `Mobile Home v3.dc.html:247` (`font-family: Arial`) was excluded as dead. No false positives to dispute — there were no findings.

The clean scan is meaningful but narrow: it proves no banned-pattern tells, not that the design works.

# Overall Impression

The visual foundation is real and the photography is better than the interface built on it. The hero at 393px is genuinely beautiful. But the implementation did not deliver the handoff, and the gap is concentrated in exactly the places where a ₪10,000+ contact-led purchase is won or lost: proof, specification credibility, and the contact affordance itself.

The single biggest opportunity is not a redesign. It is **wiring up what already exists on disk** — 364 product images, the certificate story, the three-step post-contact process, the no-showroom pricing argument — all specified in the handoff, all true, all currently absent.

# What's Working

1. **The 4C education strip** (`globals.css:96-103`). Gold Hebrew micro-label → tracked Latin term (correctly `dir="ltr"`) → quiet body, in a hairline four-cell grid. Teaches without patronising; carries the hierarchy with zero ornament. The one module that could not be lifted onto another product.
2. **Photography direction and the teal/paper duality.** Consistently lit, consistently art-directed with deliberate copy space, correctly split between deep-teal narrative fields and near-white catalogue fields exactly as DESIGN.md commits.
3. **RTL mechanics are genuinely authored, not bolted on.** Logical properties throughout; chevrons mirrored via `scaleX(-1)`; drawer slides from the right; `dir="ltr"` correctly applied to Latin terms, phone numbers, carat values. Confirmed: number ranges render correctly for a Hebrew reader. Focus states are clean site-wide (`*:focus-visible` with 3px outline, zero `outline:none` without replacement), every form input is labelled, and there are no console errors or 404s on any route.

# Priority Issues

### [P0] The primary contact module is invisible on every page

**What**: `.help-strip` sets `background: var(--paper)` at `globals.css:129` but sits inside `footer.site-footer` (`:135`) which sets `color: #fff`. The background is overridden; the inherited white is not. Measured **1.03:1** against AA's 4.5:1. Heading and all three tile labels (WhatsApp / שיחה / קביעת פגישה) are invisible. Renders as three empty bordered boxes.

**Why it matters**: This product's entire conversion model is contact-led, and this is the persistent contact affordance in `SiteFooter.tsx:8` — broken on all seven routes.

**Fix**: Add `color: var(--ink)` to `.help-strip` at `globals.css:129`. One line.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`

### [P1] Collection pages are dominated by a grey void

**What**: `globals.css:75`, `:114`, `:168` set `display:grid; grid-template-columns:repeat(4,1fr); gap:1px; background:var(--rule)`. Empty cells paint the container background. Measured at 1440 on `/collections/earrings`: grid box 1205×462, one 300px card, leaving a **~903×460px grey slab** that is the dominant visual element of the page. Same on `/necklaces` and `/bracelets`. The homepage featured rail shows 6 cards in 4 columns, orphaning 2 and painting a 603×460 slab.

**Why it matters**: Grey rectangles where product should be read as a broken page, not as negative space.

**Fix**: Drop `background: var(--rule)` and `gap: 1px`; move the hairlines onto the cells (`border-inline-end` / `border-block-end` on `.product-card`, `.category-tile`, `.product-gallery figure`) with `gap: 0`. Change `.product-grid` to `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(260px, 1fr))` so a one-item collection renders one card at natural width. Slice the featured list to 4 or 8, not 6.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

### [P1] Product copy contradicts product photography

**What**: `catalog.ts:70` subtitles the flagship `יהלום עגול · ארבע שיניים` (four prongs). Its own images and the homepage hero show an unambiguous **six-prong** head — verified by direct inspection of `ring-01-top.jpg`. The handoff says `שש ציפורניים`. Separately, `catalog.ts:80,94,108` build three galleries as `[own-front, ...ringGallery.slice(1)]`, so the profile/top/scale frames of the pavé, three-stone and rose-gold rings are all ring-01's plain white-gold solitaire, with alt text asserting they are the product. `commonRingSpecs` (`catalog.ts:56-62`) applies `E–F / VS1–VS2 / Excellent / IGI` to the earrings, the pendant and a 3.00ct tennis bracelet at ₪8,900 — a combination that is not credible.

**Why it matters**: These are checkable claims that fail on inspection, on the page where the purchase decision is made, for a brand whose entire pitch is "we show you the spec."

**Fix**: Correct the subtitle to `שש שיניים`; give each product its own `specs` array and remove `commonRingSpecs` from non-ring products; either source per-product gallery frames or reduce those galleries to frames that genuinely exist. Wire metal selection to the gallery from the unused `catalog/` metal-variant archive, or remove the metal selector.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] The certificate story — the entire trust proposition — is missing

**What**: `GIA` appears zero times in `src/`. `IGI` appears once, in a collapsed table. The persistent `תעודת GIA או IGI לכל אבן` strip present on all current handoff artboards was replaced with a scheduling line (`SiteHeader.tsx:33`). The handoff FAQ answer explaining how to verify a report number at the lab's site was cut; the shipped answer (`content.ts:11`) declines to name a lab at all. There is also no address, no named human, no gemologist, no studio photo, no years in business.

**Why it matters**: For a high-ticket contact-led purchase with no cart and no showroom, the verifiable certificate number is the only independent proof the business can offer — and it is non-fabricated, so `PRODUCT.md`'s honesty constraint permits it fully. The constraint appears to have been read as "include no proof" rather than "include no fabricated proof."

**Fix**: Restore the certificate strip in `SiteHeader.tsx:33`; restore the handoff's verification FAQ and the honest no-showroom pricing explanation in `content.ts`; port the Contact page's three-step `מה קורה אחרי שפונים` (call → stone and certificate → delivery) into `contact/page.tsx` and repeat it under the product CTA.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable clarify`

### [P1] The 761–1050px band is unauthored and drops a product category

**What**: All eight `.dc.html` artboards are 393px — there is no desktop or tablet design authority anywhere. At 768px, verified live: `globals.css:281` computes `צמידים` to `display:none` in the desktop nav, while `.mobile-only` drawer is also `display:none` (it only appears at ≤760px). **Bracelets is unreachable from the header across the entire 761–1050px band.** Also at 768: `.education-row` holds 4 columns so each paragraph is 116px wide (~15 characters per line), and `.product-layout` collapses the product photograph to 317×317 beside a 340px-wide, 1028px-tall spec panel — the image is smaller than the text next to it.

**Why it matters**: This band covers iPads and every 13" laptop in a non-maximised window — precisely where a high-budget comparison shopper evaluates.

**Fix**: Delete `globals.css:281` and move the drawer breakpoint to `max-width: 1050px` so no nav item is ever hidden. Move `.education-row` to 2 columns and `.difference-grid` to 1 column at 1050px rather than 760px. Apply the single-column `.product-layout` at ≤1050px so the gallery is never narrower than the panel.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

### [P2] The brand's gold fails contrast on every price

**What**: `--gold #987534` on `--paper #FCFCFA` computes to **4.15:1**, below AA's 4.5:1. It carries `.product-card strong` (every price, 14px), `.product-category` (12px), `.education-row span` (13px), and the `/about` blockquote.

**Why it matters**: The brand colour fails on the brand's most important use, and it is an IS 5568 exposure for an Israeli commercial site.

**Fix**: Darken `--gold` to approximately `#8A6A2F` (≈4.6:1) for text use while keeping the lighter tone for rules and non-text accents, or raise the affected text to 18.66px bold / 24px where the 3:1 large-text threshold applies.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable audit`

# Corrections to the Assessments

Two claims from the design review did not survive verification and are excluded from the findings above:

1. **"Hebrew number ranges render reversed."** The review reported that `א׳–ה׳ 09:00–18:00` displays as `18:00–09:00`, and that the studio therefore advertises overnight hours. Measured per-character x-positions in the live DOM: `09:00` sits at x=660 and `18:00` at x=623, so `09:00` is to the **right** of `18:00`. A Hebrew reader scanning right-to-left encounters `09:00` first. **The rendering is correct** — this is standard bidi behaviour, and reading the line left-to-right (as the review did) is the error. The same reasoning covers the budget `option` values and the `10–21` lead time. No fix needed.

2. **Gold contrast ratio.** The review computed 4.21:1; the measured and independently recalculated value is **4.15:1**. Both fail AA; the reported figure is corrected.

Additionally, an early observation of mine — clipped text and a horizontal scrollbar at 393px — was **disproved**. Document width is clean at 320/393/768/1024/1440. The clipping is `.category-grid` and `.featured-rail`, intentional `overflow-x:auto` rails that correctly become CSS grid at ≥768px. Not a defect.

# Questions to Consider

1. You are sitting on 364 real product images with metal variants and a four-version try-on archive. Why is the site selling seven invented products? What decision made the fictional catalogue the shipping catalogue?
2. If the answer to "why buy here instead of a mall" is the rent argument the handoff spells out — the only genuinely differentiating claim you own, and a true one — why does the shipped site not say it anywhere?
3. Did `PRODUCT.md`'s ban on fabricated proof get read as "include no proof"? Certificate verification, the no-showroom explanation, the three-step post-contact process and the studio's address are all true and all absent.
4. Every artboard is 393px. Who decided the desktop grid, and against what? The two widths where a ₪35,000 decision actually gets made have never been art-directed.
5. The configurator lets a buyer choose metal, carat and ring size but not natural versus lab — a 3–5× price variable — while `/diamond-search` pre-selects lab for them. Considered merchandising position, or omission?
6. What does a buyer do at 22:00 on a Saturday when WhatsApp isn't the answer? Every conversion path is one channel with no fallback and no server ever receiving a form.
