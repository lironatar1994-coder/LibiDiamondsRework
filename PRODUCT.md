# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, server-rendered public pages, and PM2 on Linux.

## Users

Hebrew-speaking buyers in Israel comparing diamond jewelry, especially engagement rings, who want transparent specifications, a calm way to browse, and direct expert help before purchasing.

## Product Purpose

LIBI Diamonds presents diamond jewelry, explains the important buying decisions, and turns qualified interest into a personal consultation through WhatsApp or phone.

## Positioning

An appointment-led diamond studio with a direct, low-overhead buying experience: clear specifications and prices, then human guidance rather than an anonymous cart checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public catalog, product configuration, diamond-search brief, FAQ, contact and legal information.
- Hebrew-first RTL interface; prices use Israeli shekels and contact routes use the existing Israeli phone number.
- Product availability and final price are confirmed personally. The site must not imply that a generated certificate image is a certificate for a specific stone.
- No fabricated reviews, customer counts, laboratory reports, or certification identifiers.

## Brand Commitments

The Claude design handoff in this project is the visual source of truth. Preserve its warm-white catalogue field, deep-teal editorial photography, graphite type, restrained gold, fine rules, light Heebo typography, and direct Hebrew voice.

## Evidence on Hand

- Six supplied page designs and their working copy at project root.
- 27 approved design assets under `public/images/design`.
- Protected product-media archive under `public/images/products` and `public/try-on`.

## Product Principles

- Make the product and its specification easy to understand before asking for contact.
- Use real supplied imagery and state uncertainty honestly.
- Keep consultation one action away on every commercial page.
- Serve complete semantic content to search engines without depending on client-side rendering.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implement Hebrew RTL semantics and IS 5568/WCAG AA fundamentals: semantic landmarks, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, contrast, descriptive alternatives, and accessible form status.
