# Image prompts — copy/paste queue

20 files. Each block is complete: paste the PROMPT, paste the NEGATIVE, save under the
FILE name given. Generate in the order listed — 01 and 10 set the look for the rest.

The reasoning behind these lives in `image-brief.md`. This file is just the queue.

## The negative prompt — paste with EVERY image

```
no people, no person, no hands, no fingers, no fingernails, no skin, no arms,
no neck, no ears, no face, no body, no model, no mannequin, no bust, no display
head, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no signature,
no price tag, no packaging box, no gift box, no ribbon, no flowers, no petals,

no collage, no split screen
```

## The three constants — true in every prompt below

- **Surface:** deep teal-blue silk, roughly `#17313A`, soft irregular folds, falling
  to near-black in shadow. Bottle-glass teal, not navy, not royal, not turquoise.
- **Light:** one soft key from the upper right at 45°, no fill light, deep falloff
  to the lower left.
- **Lens:** 100mm macro at f/2.8, shallow depth of field, sharp on the stone.

```

cluster of white fire; the white gold band shows one long soft specular
```

**Reject if:** the bottom third has any highlight or fold detail · the stone reads
grey or glassy rather than white and alive · the metal looks like chrome or plastic
· more than one light source · the silk reads brown, royal blue or bright turquoise
· the ring is dead-centre instead of slightly right.

## Do not generate

The product page lets clients switch **origin** (טבעי / מעבדה), **carat**, and
**ring size**. None of those needs its own photograph — it is the same physical
setting in every case, and only the stone data and price change. Generating six
carat variants or a separate lab version is 12 wasted files, and a lab-versus-
natural photo pair implies a visible difference that does not exist.

## After generating

1. Save each file under the exact path and name above. The design placeholders
   carry these names on screen — match them character for character.
2. Check 01 on a phone at 393px: the headline must stay fully legible over the
   bottom third. If not, reshoot — do not darken the gradient, which flattens the
   whole image.
3. Put 07 and 08 side by side at actual size. If one looks better than the other,
   regenerate the weaker one.
4. Files 10–14 and 18–20 sit beside specific certificate numbers and prices. A
   generated image is not the item you ship. For anything a client can enquire
   about by cert number, a phone in a lightbox on the same teal silk beats
   generation — and the shared surface is what lets those shots sit convincingly
   beside the generated ones.
